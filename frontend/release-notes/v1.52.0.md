# What's New in Version 1.52.0

**Released:** November 17, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Work available to claim

When a task or process is waiting for someone to take it, the Personnel Contact and Assistant get an alert.

**What you can do:**
- See a phone alert, or a browser alert if you already allowed notifications
- See how many tasks are waiting on Work
- Open the task from the alert and claim it

**How to use it:**
1. Allow alerts when the phone or the browser asks.
2. When work is added with no one assigned, or returned to the pool, the alert arrives.
3. Open it and choose Claim.

Several tasks added at once become one alert.

### Remove a task

An administrator, a Personnel Contact or Assistant, or the person who submitted a task can take it off the list.

**How to use it:**
1. Open Work.
2. Choose Remove on the task.

### Edit a project date

Personnel can change or remove a date on a project.

**How to use it:**
1. Open the project.
2. Edit the date, or remove it.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The person who put the work in the pool does not get the alert. Support does not get it.
- The phone count on Available, and Claim from an alert, need the next app update from the store.
- The extra card on the dashboard is gone. Open work is still on My Work.
