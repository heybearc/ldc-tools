# What's New in Version 1.54.3

**Released:** November 18, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Find the work you need

On Work, choose a project to see only that project's tasks. Choose Overdue to see open tasks whose date has passed. Choose Email off to see open tasks that will not email the requestor when they are marked done.

**How to use it:**
1. Open Work.
2. Choose a project, Overdue, or Email off.
3. For tasks that should email the requestor, select them and choose Email on.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Finished tasks follow the project you choose.
- A task dated today is not overdue.
- No action is required for tasks that should stay silent.
