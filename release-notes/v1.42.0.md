# What's New in Version 1.42.0

**Released:** October 30, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Phone layout

Open the menu at the top of the screen. Directory crews list one role under the next. Chat shows the conversation list, then the thread. Help and release notes stay on the screen.

### Directory contacts

Add person on a trade or crew puts them on the directory. That does not create a sign-in account. If they already have an account, the role stays on that account.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action is required.
- People who already sign in keep their accounts.
