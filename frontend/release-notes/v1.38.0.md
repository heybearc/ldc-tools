# What's New in Version 1.38.0

**Released:** October 30, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Invite someone

User Management has a form at the top. Enter a name, email, and access level, then send the invitation. They receive an email with a link to set up their account. The link expires in 7 days.

**What you can do:**
- Send an invitation without opening a separate window
- Resend the email from a person who is still Invited

**How to use it:**
1. Open Admin, then User Management
2. Fill in the invite form
3. Choose Send invitation email

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Notices instead of popups

Cancel, resend, delete, and password reset ask you to confirm in a notice at the corner of the screen.

If the invitation email cannot be sent, the account is not created.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Email must be configured under Admin → Email Configuration
- A person marked Invited has not finished setup. Use Resend Invitation if they need the email again
