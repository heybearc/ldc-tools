# What's New in Version 1.48.6

**Released:** November 11, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Created accounts stay on the Accounts list

Someone you create in Admin → Accounts shows up in the list right away, even if they have not finished setting up their sign-in. You can send them an invitation from that row.

### Invite or update several people at once

Each account has a checkbox. Select the people you want, then choose Send invitations. You can also change their access, status, or construction group and choose Apply.

### Add a person to Personnel

Add person on the directory can place someone on Personnel, as well as on the construction group or a trade. Adding them there does not create a sign-in account.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Open Admin → Accounts to find someone who was created but has not signed in yet
- Select their row and choose Send invitations, or open More actions and choose Send invitation
- A notice asks you to confirm before the email is sent
