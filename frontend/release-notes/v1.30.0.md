# What's New in Version 1.30.0

**Released:** October 28, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Place a profile photo

Open a person and drag their photo until the face sits in the circle. Use the zoom slider if you need a closer crop. Save stores that view. The directory and trade team cards show the saved photo.

**What you can do:**
- Move the photo inside the circle
- Zoom in or out
- Replace or remove the photo

**How to use it:**
1. Open the directory and click the person.
2. Drag the photo so the face sits in the circle.
3. Save.

### Open one trade team

The home page is the trade leadership directory. Trade Teams lists each specialty. Open a team to see its crews and the people in each oversight role. The directory has an Open team link on each trade.

**How to use it:**
1. Open Directory or Trade Teams.
2. Open a team.
3. Click a name to edit that person. Add person adds someone to the team or a crew.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Photos already in the directory stay as they are until you replace or reposition one and save.
- A name-only save does not change the photo.
