# What's New in Version 1.54.1

**Released:** November 17, 2026

---

## 🔧 Improvements

### Store badges in the footer

After you sign in, the footer shows Download on the App Store and Get it on Google Play. The same badges are on the Mobile app page.

**How to use it:**
1. Sign in at ldctools.com.
2. At the bottom of the page, choose the store badge for your phone.
3. Install the app, then sign in with the same LDC Tools account.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Downloading the app does not create an account.
- On Android, use the Google account that was added to the test.
