# What's New in Version 1.45.1

**Released:** November 3, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Turn notifications off

When notifications are already on for this browser, the Chat button says Turn off. Choose it to stop notifications on this phone or computer.

### Stay on the chat you opened

Opening Chat shows the list until you pick a conversation. Switching conversations keeps the messages for the one you are reading. A step's Reference link no longer checks the step off.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- Refresh Chat if the button still says Turn on after you already enabled notifications
