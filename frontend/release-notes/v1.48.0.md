# What's New in Version 1.48.0

**Released:** November 8, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Phone alerts

The phone app can show an alert, a badge, and a sound for a new chat message or an announcement. Allow alerts when the phone asks.

### Processes, projects, and trade teams

The phone app can start a process, open a project, and list trade teams and crews. Directory, feedback, and admin tools that were already on the website are in the app as well.

---

## 🔧 Improvements

### Completion email

When a task is marked done, the page says whether the completion email was sent.
