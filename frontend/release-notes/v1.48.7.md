# What's New in Version 1.48.7

**Released:** November 11, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Phone numbers look the same everywhere

When you enter a phone number on a person, a volunteer, or a crew request, it is shown as (555) 555-5555. The same form appears in the directory and in the phone app.

A partial number, such as a few digits, is not saved. Leave the phone blank when you do not have one.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Type the 10-digit number. The parentheses and dash are added for you on the website.
- Numbers already saved are shown in this form when they are a real US number.
- No action is required for numbers that were already correct.
