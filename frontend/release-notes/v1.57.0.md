# What's New in Version 1.57.0

**Released:** November 27, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Accounts are for Personnel

A person added to the construction group directory no longer gets a sign-in account. They stay in the directory, on their trade team, and on their crew. Sign-in is for the Personnel team.

**What you can do:**
- Add a directory person without creating an account
- Keep Personnel as the people who sign in

### Work alerts go to Personnel

When work is waiting, or when someone assigns work, the email and the phone alert go to Personnel in that construction group. Directory people are not mailed.
