# What's New in Version 1.29.0

**Released:** October 27, 2026

## Move someone to another role

Open a person on the directory and change their trade, crew, or role. The directory buttons, including Export CSV, stay on the page.

- Move a person between trades
- Move a person between a trade and a crew
- Change their role

## Sisters stay in Support

Mark a woman as a sister. She can only be Trade Team Support or Trade Crew Support.

Check Sister on each woman. People are not marked automatically.

## Directory buttons

Export CSV is no longer cut off at the edge of the page.
