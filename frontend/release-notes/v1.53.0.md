# What's New in Version 1.53.0

**Released:** November 17, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### A link on an announcement

An announcement can include a web address. People open it from the notice on the website, or from Open link in the app.

**What you can do:**
- Leave the link blank when the message stands on its own
- Paste a full address that starts with https://
- Choose Global for the whole app, or one construction group

**How to use it:**
1. Open Admin and choose Announcements.
2. Choose New Announcement.
3. Write the title and message.
4. Paste the web link if you have one, then create the announcement.

### Reset a password from sign-in

If you cannot remember your password, ask for a reset from the sign-in screen. The new password is chosen only after you open the email.

**How to use it:**
1. On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password?
2. Enter the email on your account.
3. Open the link in that email. It expires in one hour.
4. Choose a new password, then sign in.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The sign-in screen always says a reset email is on its way, even when that address has no account.
- An announcement is no longer limited to selected roles. Everyone in the chosen audience sees it.
- No other action is required.
