# What's New in Version 1.38.1

**Released:** October 30, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Resend an invitation

If someone is already marked Invited, choose More actions and then Resend Invitation. Confirm the notice, and they receive the setup email. The link expires in 7 days.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Use Resend Invitation for a person already on the list
- The invite form is for someone who does not have an account yet
