# What's New in Version 1.47.0

**Released:** November 5, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Privacy policy and support

Anyone can open the privacy policy and the support page. The app is invite-only. Downloading it does not create an account. Request access from the support page.

### Delete your sign-in

On My Work in the phone app, Delete account turns off your sign-in and removes your password. The construction group keeps the crew records it already has.

### Report or block in chat

Report sends a message to the administrators. Block hides that person's messages.
