# What's New in Version 1.31.0

**Released:** October 29, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Construction group and ancillary contacts

The directory lists three groups for your construction group. Trades stay as they were. Above them you now see Construction group contacts (Contact, Assistant, and Support) and ancillary region people.

**What you can do:**
- Find a construction group contact without leaving the directory
- Copy their email
- Export those people with the trade list

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Saving a person no longer asks you to choose an image file when the photo is already on file. Zoom only when you want a new crop.

## 🔧 Improvements

- The admin screen for editing the branch, zone, and region tree is gone. A signed-in person belongs to the construction group where they serve.

## 📝 Notes

- Ancillary stays empty until someone has a region-support or construction-group staff role that is not Personnel.
