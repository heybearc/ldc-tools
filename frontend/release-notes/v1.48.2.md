# What's New in Version 1.48.2

**Released:** November 8, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Messages go only to real addresses

A completion notice or an email test is not sent when the address cannot receive mail, such as an example or local address. Your inbox is not filled with bounce notices from those tests.

### The phone matches the website

The people who can add a project, a trade team, or a crew on the phone are the same people who can do that on the website. Personnel still add a process.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- Install the updated phone app to add a project or trade team from the phone
